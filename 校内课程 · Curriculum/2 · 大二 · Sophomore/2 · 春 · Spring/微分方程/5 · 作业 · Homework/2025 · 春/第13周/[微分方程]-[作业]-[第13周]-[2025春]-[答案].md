# 微分方程 第13周作业答案（2025春）

## 第 1 题

### 题目

求下列初值问题：

$$
\begin{cases}
x' + x - y = \mathrm{e}^t, \\
y' + 3x - 2y = 2\mathrm{e}^t, \\
x(0) = y(0) = 1.
\end{cases}
$$

### 答案

**答案：** 设 $\mathcal{L}\{x(t)\} = X(s)$， $\mathcal{L}\{y(t)\} = Y(s)$，对两式作 Laplace 变换：

$$
\begin{cases}
sX(s) - x(0) + X(s) - Y(s) = \dfrac{1}{s-1} \quad ③ \\
sY(s) - y(0) + 3X(s) - 2Y(s) = \dfrac{2}{s-1} \quad ④
\end{cases}
$$

其中 $x(0) = y(0) = 1$。解得

$$
X(s) = \frac{1}{s-1},\qquad Y(s) = \frac{1}{s-1},
$$

故 $x(t) = y(t) = \mathrm{e}^t$， $t > 0$。

## 第 3 题

### 题目

$$
\begin{cases}
(2x'' - x' + 9x) - (y'' + y' + 3y) = 0, \\
(2x'' + x' + 7x) - (y'' - y' + 5y) = 0, \\
x(0) = x'(0) = 1,\quad y(0) = y'(0) = 0.
\end{cases}
$$

### 答案

**答案：** 对 ①、② 作下列变形：令 $③ = \dfrac{① + ②}{2}$， $④ = \dfrac{② - ①}{2}$，于是 $③ + ④ = ②$， $③ - ④ = ①$。③、④ 合并 ①、② 得

$$
\begin{cases}
2x'' + 8x = y'' + 4y \quad ③ \\
x' - x = -y' + y \quad ④
\end{cases}
$$

对 ③、④ 作 Laplace 变换：

$$
2\left(s^2 X(s) - sx(0) - x'(0)\right) + 8X(s) = s^2 Y(s) - sy(0) - y'(0) + 4Y(s) \quad ⑤
$$

$$
sX(s) - x(0) - X(s) = -sY(s) + y(0) + Y(s) \quad ⑥
$$

整理并代入初值：

$$
\begin{cases}
2(s^2 + 4)X(s) - 2(s + 1) = (s^2 + 4)Y(s) \quad ⑤ \\
(s - 1)X(s) - 1 = -(s - 1)Y(s) \quad ⑥
\end{cases}
$$

解得

$$
X(s) = \frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{2}{s^2 + 4} + \frac{2s}{s^2 + 4} + \frac{1}{s - 1}\right),
$$

$$
Y(s) = \frac{2}{3}\left(\frac{1}{s - 1} - \frac{s}{s^2 + 4} - \frac{1}{s^2 + 4}\right).
$$
故

$$
x(t) = \frac{1}{3}\sin 2t + \frac{2}{3}\cos 2t + \frac{1}{3}\mathrm{e}^t,
$$

$$
y(t) = \frac{2}{3}\mathrm{e}^t - \frac{2}{3}\cos 2t - \frac{1}{3}\sin 2t,\qquad t > 0.
$$

## 第 5 题

### 题目

$$
\begin{cases}
x' = y, \\
y' = z, \\
z' = -6x - 11y - 6z + \mathrm{e}^t, \\
x(0) = y(0) = z(0) = 0.
\end{cases}
$$

### 答案

**答案：** 设 $\mathcal{L}\{x(t)\} = X(s)$， $\mathcal{L}\{y(t)\} = Y(s)$， $\mathcal{L}\{z(t)\} = Z(s)$，对 ①、②、③ 作 Laplace 变换（已代入 $x(0) = y(0) = z(0) = 0$）：

$$
\begin{cases}
sX(s) = Y(s) \quad ④ \\
sY(s) = Z(s) \quad ⑤ \\
sZ(s) = -6X(s) - 11Y(s) - 6Z(s) + \dfrac{1}{s - 1} \quad ⑥
\end{cases}
$$

④、⑤ 代入 ⑥，有

$$
Z(s) = \frac{s^2}{(s + 3)(s + 2)(s + 1)(s - 1)} = -\frac{9}{8}\cdot\frac{1}{s + 3} + \frac{4}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{s + 2} - \frac{1}{4}\cdot\frac{1}{s + 1} + \frac{1}{24}\cdot\frac{1}{s - 1},
$$

$$
Y(s) = \frac{s}{(s + 3)(s + 2)(s + 1)(s - 1)} = \frac{3}{8}\cdot\frac{1}{s + 3} - \frac{2}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{s + 2} + \frac{1}{4}\cdot\frac{1}{s + 1} + \frac{1}{24}\cdot\frac{1}{s - 1},
$$

$$
X(s) = \frac{1}{(s + 3)(s + 2)(s + 1)(s - 1)} = -\frac{1}{8}\cdot\frac{1}{s + 3} + \frac{1}{3}\cdot\frac{1}{s + 2} - \frac{1}{4}\cdot\frac{1}{s + 1} + \frac{1}{24}\cdot\frac{1}{s - 1}.
$$

故

$$
\begin{cases}
x(t) = -\dfrac{1}{8}\mathrm{e}^{-3t} + \dfrac{1}{3}\mathrm{e}^{-2t} - \dfrac{1}{4}\mathrm{e}^{-t} + \dfrac{1}{24}\mathrm{e}^t, \\
y(t) = \dfrac{3}{8}\mathrm{e}^{-3t} - \dfrac{2}{3}\mathrm{e}^{-2t} + \dfrac{1}{4}\mathrm{e}^{-t} + \dfrac{1}{24}\mathrm{e}^t, \qquad (t > 0) \\
z(t) = -\dfrac{9}{8}\mathrm{e}^{-3t} + \dfrac{4}{3}\mathrm{e}^{-2t} - \dfrac{1}{4}\mathrm{e}^{-t} + \dfrac{1}{24}\mathrm{e}^t.
\end{cases}
$$
