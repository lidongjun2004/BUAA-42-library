# G28组论文修改

## Origin Version

**Frequent pattern discovery (FP discovery) is part of knowledge in databases, Massive Online Analysis, and data mining, it is the process of identifying patterns or associations within a dataset occur frequently.** However, as the number of items or variables in a dataset increases, the number of possible patterns grows exponentially. Meanwhile, a combinatorial explosion will occur when try to enumerate all possible combinations of items. **Hence, frequent closed pattern (FCP) has been raised up, to solve these problems. Frequent closed patterns are a specific subset of frequent patterns. A frequent closed pattern is a maximal frequent pattern, meaning that it cannot be extended by adding more items while maintaining the same frequency.**

The FP-Growth algorithm in data mining is a popular method for frequent pattern mining. The algorithm is efficient for mining frequent item sets in large datasets. However, the FCP may be more useful than just frequent patterns in some cases. The FCP not only includes frequent patterns themselves but also all supersets of frequent patterns. **Therefore, when mining association rules, it can provide more comprehensive results. Moreover, association rules generated by FCP are often more specific and precise because it considers more pattern combinations, enabling the discovery of deeper association relationships.**
