## 习题六

### 习题六-1

#### 题目

证明：若 $\int f(x)\mathrm{d}x = F(x) + C$，则
$$\int f(ax + b)\mathrm{d}x = \frac{1}{a}F(ax + b) + C \quad (a \neq 0).$$

#### 答案

**答案：** 由 $\int f(ax)\mathrm{d}x = F(ax) + C$ 得 $\dfrac{\mathrm{d}F(x)}{\mathrm{d}x} = f(x)$，于是

$$\frac{\mathrm{d}F(ax+b)}{\mathrm{d}x}=\frac{\mathrm{d}F(ax+b)}{\mathrm{d}(ax+b)}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{d}(ax+b)}{\mathrm{d}x}$$

（原稿此处的 $=f(ax+b)\cdot a$ 已被划去）故

$$\frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}x}\left[\frac1aF(ax+b)\right]=f(ax+b),$$

所以

$$\int f(ax+b)\mathrm{d}x=\frac1aF(ax+b)+C.$$

### **习题六-2**

#### 题目

求下列不定积分：

**(1)** $\int (\sqrt{2x} + \sqrt[3]{3x})\mathrm{d}x$;

**(2)** $\int \left(1 + \frac{1}{x^2}\sqrt{x\sqrt{x}}\right)\mathrm{d}x$;

**(3)** $\int \left(3^{x+1} + 3^{-x} + \frac{1}{3}\mathrm{e}^x\right)\mathrm{d}x$;

**(4)** $\int \frac{2 - \sin^2 x}{\cos^2 x}\mathrm{d}x$;

**(5)** $\int \left(\sqrt{\frac{1 + x}{1 - x}} + \sqrt{\frac{1 - x}{1 + x}}\right)\mathrm{d}x$;

**(6)** $\int \frac{\cos 2x}{\cos^2 x \sin^2 x}\mathrm{d}x$;

**(7)** $\int \sqrt{1 - \sin 2x}\mathrm{d}x$;

**(8)** $\int (x + |x|)\mathrm{d}x$;

**(9)** $\int \operatorname{sgn}(\sin x)\mathrm{d}x$;

**(10)** $\int [x]\mathrm{d}x$.

#### 答案

##### **习题六-2(1)**

**答案：**

$$\int(\sqrt{2x}+\sqrt[3]{3x})\mathrm{d}x=\sqrt{2}\int x^{1/2}\mathrm{d}x+\sqrt[3]{3}\int x^{1/3}\mathrm{d}x=\frac{2\sqrt{2}}{3}x^{3/2}+\frac{3\sqrt[3]{3}}{4}x^{4/3}+C$$

#### **习题六-2(2)**

**答案：**

$$\int\left(1+\frac{1}{x^{2}}\sqrt{x\sqrt{x}}\right)\mathrm{d}x=\int\mathrm{d}x+\int\frac{x^{3/4}}{x^{2}}\mathrm{d}x=\int\mathrm{d}x+\int x^{-5/4}\mathrm{d}x=x-4x^{-1/4}+C$$

#### **习题六-2(3)**

**答案：**

$$\int\left(3^{x+1}+3^{-x}+\frac{1}{3}\mathrm{e}^{x}\right)\mathrm{d}x=3\int 3^{x}\mathrm{d}x+\int\left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^{x}\mathrm{d}x+\frac{1}{3}\int\mathrm{e}^{x}\mathrm{d}x=\frac{3}{\ln 3}3^{x}+\frac{(1/3)^{x}}{\ln (1/3)}+\frac{1}{3}\mathrm{e}^{x}+C$$

#### **习题六-2(4)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{2-\sin^{2}x}{\cos^{2}x}\mathrm{d}x=\int\frac{1+\cos^{2}x}{\cos^{2}x}\mathrm{d}x=\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\cos^{2}x}+\int\mathrm{d}x=\tan x+x+C$$

#### **习题六-2(5)**

**答案：**

$$\int\left(\sqrt{\frac{1+x}{1-x}}+\sqrt{\frac{1-x}{1+x}}\right)\mathrm{d}x=\int\left(\frac{\sqrt{1+x}}{\sqrt{1-x}}+\frac{\sqrt{1-x}}{\sqrt{1+x}}\right)\mathrm{d}x=\int\frac{(1+x)+(1-x)}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}\mathrm{d}x=\int\frac{2\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}=2\arcsin x+C$$

#### **习题六-2(6)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{\cos 2x}{\cos^{2}x\sin^{2}x}\mathrm{d}x=\int\frac{\cos^{2}x-\sin^{2}x}{\cos^{2}x\sin^{2}x}\mathrm{d}x=\int\left(\frac{1}{\sin^{2}x}-\frac{1}{\cos^{2}x}\right)\mathrm{d}x=\int\csc^{2}x\,\mathrm{d}x-\int\sec^{2}x\,\mathrm{d}x=-\cot x-\tan x+C$$

#### **习题六-2(7)**

**答案：**

$$\int\sqrt{1-\sin 2x}\,\mathrm{d}x=\int\sqrt{1-2\sin x\cos x}\,\mathrm{d}x=\int\sqrt{(\sin x-\cos x)^{2}}\,\mathrm{d}x=\int\pm(\sin x-\cos x)\mathrm{d}x=\pm(-\cos x-\sin x)+C$$

#### **习题六-2(8)**

**答案：**

$$\int(x+|x|)\mathrm{d}x=\begin{cases}\displaystyle\int 2x\,\mathrm{d}x=x^{2}+C, & x\geq 0\\[6pt] \displaystyle\int 0\,\mathrm{d}x=C, & x<0\end{cases}$$

#### **习题六-2(9)**

**答案：**

$$\int\operatorname{sgn}(\sin x)\mathrm{d}x=\begin{cases}x+C, & x\in[2k\pi,(2k+1)\pi]\\[6pt] -x+C, & x\in[(2k-1)\pi,2k\pi]\end{cases}$$

#### **习题六-2(10)**

**答案：**

$$\int[x]\mathrm{d}x=\int n\,\mathrm{d}x=nx+C,\quad x\in[n,n+1)=x[x]+C$$

### **习题六-3**

#### 题目

用换元法求下列不定积分：

**(1)** $\int \frac{2\mathrm{d}x}{3 - 5x}$;

**(2)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{2 - x^2}}$;

**(3)** $\int x\sqrt[3]{1 - 3x}\mathrm{d}x$;

**(4)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{1 + \sin x}$;

**(5)** $\int x(1 + x^2)^5\mathrm{d}x$;

**(6)** $\int \frac{x\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{1 - x^2}}$;

**(7)** $\int \frac{x\mathrm{d}x}{4 + x^4}$;

**(8)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{e}^x}{1 + \mathrm{e}^x}\mathrm{d}x$;

**(9)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{x}(1 + x)}$;

**(10)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x\sqrt{x^2 - 1}}$;

**(11)** $\int \sin x \sin 3x\mathrm{d}x$;

**(12)** $\int \sin^2 x \cos^2 x\mathrm{d}x$;

**(13)** $\int \frac{\sin 2x}{\sqrt{1 + \sin^2 x}}\mathrm{d}x$;

**(14)** $\int \frac{\sin x \cos x}{\sqrt{a^2 \sin^2 x + b^2 \cos^2 x}}\mathrm{d}x (a^2 > b^2)$;

**(15)** $\int \frac{\sqrt{a^2 - x^2}}{x}\mathrm{d}x$;

**(16)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^2\sqrt{1 + x^2}}$;

**(17)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{(a^2 + x^2)^3}}$;

**(18)** $\int \frac{x}{1 + \sqrt{x}}\mathrm{d}x$;

**(19)** $\int \sqrt{\frac{2 + x}{2 - x}}\mathrm{d}x$;

**(20)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x\sqrt{4 - x^2}}$.

#### 答案

##### **习题六-3(1)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{2\mathrm{d}x}{3-5x}=\int\frac{2\,\mathrm{d}(3-5x)}{(-5)(3-5x)}=-\frac{2}{5}\ln|3-5x|+C$$

#### **习题六-3(2)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{2-x^{2}}}=\int\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{1-(x/\sqrt{2})^{2}}}=\int\frac{\mathrm{d}(x/\sqrt{2})}{\sqrt{1-(x/\sqrt{2})^{2}}}=\arcsin\frac{x}{\sqrt{2}}+C$$

#### **习题六-3(3)**

**答案：**

$$\int x\sqrt[3]{1-3x}\,\mathrm{d}x\xlongequal{1-3x=t}\int\frac{1}{3}(1-t)t^{1/3}\left(-\frac{1}{3}\right)\mathrm{d}t=-\frac{1}{9}\int\left(t^{1/3}-t^{4/3}\right)\mathrm{d}t=-\frac{1}{9}\left[\frac{3}{4}t^{4/3}-\frac{3}{7}t^{7/3}\right]+C=-\frac{1}{12}(1-3x)^{4/3}+\frac{1}{21}(1-3x)^{7/3}+C$$

#### **习题六-3(4)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{1+\sin x}=\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\left(\cos\frac{x}{2}+\sin\frac{x}{2}\right)^{2}}=\int\frac{2\,\mathrm{d}\frac{x}{2}}{\cos^{2}\frac{x}{2}\left(1+\tan\frac{x}{2}\right)^{2}}=\int\frac{2\,\mathrm{d}\left(\tan\frac{x}{2}+1\right)}{\left(1+\tan\frac{x}{2}\right)^{2}}=-\frac{2}{1+\tan\frac{x}{2}}+C$$

#### **习题六-3(5)**

**答案：**

$$\int x(1+x^{2})^{5}\mathrm{d}x=\frac{1}{2}\int(1+x^{2})^{5}\,\mathrm{d}(x^{2}+1)=\frac{1}{12}(1+x^{2})^{6}+C$$

#### **习题六-3(6)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{x\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}=-\sqrt{1-x^{2}}+C$$

#### **习题六-3(7)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{x\mathrm{d}x}{4+x^{4}}=\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}(x^{2})}{4+x^{4}}\xlongequal{x^{2}=t}\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}t}{4+t^{2}}=\int\frac{\mathrm{d}(t/2)}{4\left(1+(t/2)^{2}\right)}=\frac{1}{4}\arctan\frac{t}{2}+C=\frac{1}{4}\arctan\frac{x^{2}}{2}+C$$

#### **习题六-3(8)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{\mathrm{e}^{x}}{1+\mathrm{e}^{x}}\mathrm{d}x=\int\frac{\mathrm{d}(\mathrm{e}^{x}+1)}{\mathrm{e}^{x}+1}=\ln(\mathrm{e}^{x}+1)+C$$

#### **习题六-3(9)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{x}(1+x)}=\int\frac{2\,\mathrm{d}\sqrt{x}}{1+x}=2\int\frac{\mathrm{d}\sqrt{x}}{1+(\sqrt{x})^{2}}=2\arctan\sqrt{x}+C$$

#### **习题六-3(10)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x\sqrt{x^{2}-1}}=\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^{2}\sqrt{1-\frac{1}{x^{2}}}}=\int\frac{-\mathrm{d}\frac{1}{x}}{\sqrt{1-\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)^{2}}}=-\arcsin\frac{1}{x}+C$$

#### **习题六-3(11)**

**答案：**

$$\int\sin x\sin 3x\,\mathrm{d}x=\int\frac{1}{2}(\cos 2x-\cos 4x)\mathrm{d}x=\frac{1}{2}\int\cos 2x\,\frac{\mathrm{d}2x}{2}-\frac{1}{2}\int\cos 4x\,\frac{\mathrm{d}4x}{4}=\frac{1}{4}\sin 2x-\frac{1}{8}\sin 4x+C$$

#### **习题六-3(12)**

**答案：**

$$\int\sin^{2}x\cos^{2}x\,\mathrm{d}x=\int\left(\frac{1}{2}\sin 2x\right)^{2}\mathrm{d}x=\frac{1}{4}\int\sin^{2}2x\,\mathrm{d}x=\frac{1}{4}\int\frac{1-\cos 4x}{2}\mathrm{d}x=\frac{x}{8}-\frac{1}{32}\sin 4x+C$$

#### **习题六-3(13)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{\sin 2x}{\sqrt{1+\sin^{2}x}}\mathrm{d}x=\int\frac{2\sin x\cos x\,\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{1+\sin^{2}x}}=\int\frac{2\sin x\,\mathrm{d}\sin x}{\sqrt{1+\sin^{2}x}}=\int\frac{\mathrm{d}\sin^{2}x}{\sqrt{1+\sin^{2}x}}=2\sqrt{1+\sin^{2}x}+C$$

#### **习题六-3(14)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{\sin x\cos x}{\sqrt{a^{2}\sin^{2}x+b^{2}\cos^{2}x}}\mathrm{d}x\ (a^{2}>b^{2})=\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}\sin^{2}x}{\sqrt{a^{2}\sin^{2}x+b^{2}\cos^{2}x}}=\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}\sin^{2}x}{\sqrt{a^{2}\sin^{2}x+b^{2}-b^{2}\sin^{2}x}}=\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{a^{2}-b^{2}}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}\left[(a^{2}-b^{2})\sin^{2}x+b^{2}\right]}{\sqrt{(a^{2}-b^{2})\sin^{2}x+b^{2}}}=\frac{1}{a^{2}-b^{2}}\sqrt{(a^{2}-b^{2})\sin^{2}x+b^{2}}+C=\frac{1}{a^{2}-b^{2}}\sqrt{a^{2}\sin^{2}x+b^{2}\cos^{2}x}+C$$

#### **习题六-3(15)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{\sqrt{a^{2}-x^{2}}}{x}\mathrm{d}x\xlongequal{x=a\sin\theta}\int\frac{a\cos\theta}{a\sin\theta}\,a\cos\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta=a\int\frac{\cos^{2}\theta}{\sin\theta}\mathrm{d}\theta=a\int\frac{-\cos^{2}\theta\,\mathrm{d}\cos\theta}{1-\cos^{2}\theta}=a\int\frac{\cos^{2}\theta\,\mathrm{d}\cos\theta}{\cos^{2}\theta-1}\xlongequal{t=\cos\theta}a\int\frac{t^{2}-1+1}{t^{2}-1}\mathrm{d}t=a\int\left(1+\frac{1}{t^{2}-1}\right)\mathrm{d}t=a\left(t+\frac{1}{2}\ln\left|\frac{t-1}{t+1}\right|\right)+C=\sqrt{a^{2}-x^{2}}+\frac{a}{2}\ln\left|\frac{\sqrt{a^{2}-x^{2}}-a}{\sqrt{a^{2}-x^{2}}+a}\right|+C$$

#### **习题六-3(16)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^{2}\sqrt{1+x^{2}}}\xlongequal{x=\tan\theta}\int\frac{\sec^{2}\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta}{\tan^{2}\theta\cdot\sec\theta}=\int\frac{\sec\theta}{\tan^{2}\theta}\mathrm{d}\theta=\int\frac{\cos\theta}{\sin^{2}\theta}\mathrm{d}\theta=\int\frac{\mathrm{d}\sin\theta}{\sin^{2}\theta}=-\frac{1}{\sin\theta}+C=-\frac{\sqrt{1+x^{2}}}{x}+C$$

#### **习题六-3(17)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{(a^{2}+x^{2})^{3}}}\xlongequal{x=a\tan\theta}\int\frac{a\sec^{2}\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta}{a^{3}\sec^{3}\theta}=\frac{1}{a^{2}}\int\cos\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta=\frac{1}{a^{2}}\sin\theta+C=\frac{1}{a^{2}}\frac{x}{\sqrt{x^{2}+a^{2}}}+C$$

#### **习题六-3(18)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{x}{1+\sqrt{x}}\mathrm{d}x\xlongequal{\sqrt{x}=t,\ x=t^{2}}\int\frac{t^{2}}{1+t}\cdot 2t\,\mathrm{d}t=2\int\frac{t^{3}}{t+1}\mathrm{d}t=2\int\frac{t^{3}+1-1}{t+1}\mathrm{d}t=2\int\left[(t^{2}-t+1)-\frac{1}{t+1}\right]\mathrm{d}t=2\left(\frac{t^{3}}{3}-\frac{t^{2}}{2}+t\right)-2\ln|t+1|+C=\frac{2}{3}x^{3/2}-x+2\sqrt{x}-2\ln(1+\sqrt{x})+C$$

#### **习题六-3(19)**

**答案：**

$$\int\sqrt{\frac{2+x}{2-x}}\mathrm{d}x=\int\frac{2+x}{\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}\mathrm{d}x=\int\frac{2}{\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}\mathrm{d}x+\int\frac{x\,\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}=2\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{1-(x/2)^{2}}}+\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{(-1)\,\mathrm{d}(4-x^{2})}{\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}=2\arcsin\frac{x}{2}-\sqrt{4-x^{2}}+C$$

#### **习题六-3(20)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}=\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^{2}\sqrt{\frac{4}{x^{2}}-1}}=-\int\frac{\frac{1}{2}\,\mathrm{d}\frac{2}{x}}{\sqrt{\left(\frac{2}{x}\right)^{2}-1}}\xlongequal{\frac{2}{x}=t}-\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}t}{\sqrt{t^{2}-1}}\xlongequal{t=\sec\theta}-\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{\sec\theta\tan\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta}{\tan\theta}=-\frac{1}{2}\int\sec\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta$$

### **习题六-4**

#### 题目

用分部积分法求下列不定积分：

**(1)** $\int \ln(1 + x^2)\mathrm{d}x$;

**(2)** $\int x\mathrm{e}^{-3x}\mathrm{d}x$;

**(3)** $\int x \cos nx\mathrm{d}x$;

**(4)** $\int \arcsin x\mathrm{d}x$;

**(5)** $\int \frac{x}{\sin^2 x}\mathrm{d}x$;

**(6)** $\int \frac{\arctan x}{x^2(1 + x^2)}\mathrm{d}x$;

**(7)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{e}^{\arctan x}}{(1+x^2)^{3/2}} \mathrm{d}x$;

**(8)** $\int \frac{x^2}{(1+x^2)^2} \mathrm{d}x$;

**(9)** $\int \sin(\ln x)\mathrm{d}x$;

**(10)** $\int \mathrm{e}^x \cos^2 x \mathrm{d}x$.

#### 答案

##### **习题六-4(1)**

**答案：**

$$\int\ln(1+x^{2})\mathrm{d}x=x\ln(1+x^{2})-\int\frac{2x^{2}}{1+x^{2}}\mathrm{d}x=x\ln(1+x^{2})-2\int\left(1-\frac{1}{1+x^{2}}\right)\mathrm{d}x=x\ln(1+x^{2})-2x+2\arctan x+C$$

#### **习题六-4(2)**

**答案：**

$$\int x\mathrm{e}^{-3x}\mathrm{d}x=-\frac{1}{3}\int x\,\mathrm{d}\mathrm{e}^{-3x}=-\frac{x}{3}\mathrm{e}^{-3x}+\frac{1}{3}\int\mathrm{e}^{-3x}\mathrm{d}x=-\frac{x}{3}\mathrm{e}^{-3x}-\frac{1}{9}\mathrm{e}^{-3x}+C$$

#### **习题六-4(3)**

**答案：**

$$\int x\cos nx\,\mathrm{d}x=\frac{1}{n}\int x\,\mathrm{d}\sin nx=\frac{x}{n}\sin nx-\frac{1}{n}\int\sin nx\,\mathrm{d}x=\frac{x}{n}\sin nx+\frac{1}{n^{2}}\cos nx+C$$

#### **习题六-4(4)**

**答案：**

$$\int\arcsin x\,\mathrm{d}x=x\arcsin x-\int\frac{x\,\mathrm{d}x}{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}=x\arcsin x+\sqrt{1-x^{2}}+C$$

#### **习题六-4(5)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{x}{\sin^{2}x}\mathrm{d}x=\int x\,\mathrm{d}(-\cot x)=-x\cot x+\int\cot x\,\mathrm{d}x=-x\cot x+\ln|\sin x|+C$$

#### **习题六-4(6)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{\arctan x}{x^{2}(1+x^{2})}\mathrm{d}x=\int\arctan x\left(\frac{1}{x^{2}}-\frac{1}{1+x^{2}}\right)\mathrm{d}x=\int\frac{\arctan x}{x^{2}}\mathrm{d}x-\int\frac{\arctan x}{1+x^{2}}\mathrm{d}x=-\int\arctan x\,\mathrm{d}\frac{1}{x}-\int\arctan x\,\mathrm{d}(\arctan x)=-\frac{1}{2}(\arctan x)^{2}-\frac{1}{x}\arctan x+\int\frac{1}{x}\cdot\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{1+x^{2}}=-\frac{1}{2}(\arctan x)^{2}-\frac{1}{x}\arctan x+\int\left(\frac{1}{x}-\frac{x}{1+x^{2}}\right)\mathrm{d}x=-\frac{1}{2}(\arctan x)^{2}-\frac{1}{x}\arctan x+\ln|x|-\frac{1}{2}\ln(1+x^{2})+C$$

#### **习题六-4(7)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{\mathrm{e}^{\arctan x}}{(1+x^{2})^{3/2}}\mathrm{d}x\xlongequal{x=\tan\theta}\int\frac{\mathrm{e}^{\theta}\sec^{2}\theta}{\sec^{3}\theta}\mathrm{d}\theta=\int\mathrm{e}^{\theta}\cos\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta=\frac{1}{2}(\sin\theta+\cos\theta)\mathrm{e}^{\theta}+C=\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{e}^{\arctan x}\frac{x+1}{\sqrt{1+x^{2}}}+C$$

#### **习题六-4(8)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{x^{2}}{(1+x^{2})^{2}}\mathrm{d}x\xlongequal{x=\tan\theta}\int\frac{\tan^{2}\theta\sec^{2}\theta}{\sec^{4}\theta}\mathrm{d}\theta=\int\frac{\tan^{2}\theta}{\sec^{2}\theta}\mathrm{d}\theta=\int\sin^{2}\theta\,\mathrm{d}\theta=\int\frac{1-\cos 2\theta}{2}\mathrm{d}\theta=\frac{\theta}{2}-\frac{1}{4}\sin 2\theta+C=\frac{1}{2}\arctan x-\frac{1}{2}\frac{x}{1+x^{2}}+C$$

#### **习题六-4(9)**

**答案：**

$$\int\sin(\ln x)\mathrm{d}x=x\sin(\ln x)-\int x\cos(\ln x)\cdot\frac{1}{x}\mathrm{d}x=x\sin(\ln x)-\int\cos(\ln x)\mathrm{d}x=x\sin(\ln x)-x\cos(\ln x)+\int x\left(-\sin(\ln x)\right)\frac{1}{x}\mathrm{d}x=x\sin(\ln x)-x\cos(\ln x)-\int\sin(\ln x)\mathrm{d}x$$

$$\therefore\ \int\sin(\ln x)\mathrm{d}x=\frac{x}{2}\left(\sin(\ln x)-\cos(\ln x)\right)+C$$

#### **习题六-4(10)**

**答案：**

$$\int\mathrm{e}^{x}\cos^{2}x\,\mathrm{d}x=\int\mathrm{e}^{x}\frac{1+\cos 2x}{2}\mathrm{d}x=\frac{1}{2}\int\mathrm{e}^{x}\mathrm{d}x+\frac{1}{2}\int\mathrm{e}^{x}\cos 2x\,\mathrm{d}x=\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{e}^{x}+\frac{\mathrm{e}^{x}}{10}\left[2\sin 2x+\cos 2x\right]+C$$

### **习题六-5**

#### 题目

对下列不定积分建立递推公式:

**(1)** $I_n = \int x^m \ln^n x \mathrm{d}x$;

**(2)** $I_n = \int x^n \mathrm{e}^{-x} \mathrm{d}x$;

**(3)** $I_n = \int \frac{x^n}{\sqrt{1-x^2}} \mathrm{d}x$;

(4) $I_n = \int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^n \sqrt{1+x^2}}$.

#### 答案

##### **习题六-5(1)**

**答案：**

$$I_{m,n}=\int x^{m}\ln^{n}x\,\mathrm{d}x=\int\ln^{n}x\,\mathrm{d}\frac{x^{m+1}}{m+1}=\frac{x^{m+1}}{m+1}\ln^{n}x-\frac{1}{m+1}\int x^{m+1}\cdot n\ln^{n-1}x\cdot\frac{1}{x}\mathrm{d}x=\frac{x^{m+1}}{m+1}\ln^{n}x-\frac{n}{m+1}\int x^{m}\ln^{n-1}x\,\mathrm{d}x=\frac{x^{m+1}}{m+1}\ln^{n}x-\frac{n}{m+1}I_{m,n-1}$$

$$I_{m,0}=\int x^{m}\mathrm{d}x=\frac{x^{m+1}}{m+1}+C$$

$$I_{m,n}=\frac{x^{m+1}}{m+1}\ln^{n}x-\frac{n x^{m+1}}{(m+1)^{2}}\ln^{n-1}x+\frac{n(n-1)}{(m+1)^{2}}I_{m,n-2}=\cdots$$

把递推式反复代入 $I_{m,0}=\dfrac{x^{m+1}}{m+1}+C$，得

$$I_{m,n}=\frac{x^{m+1}}{m+1}\left[\ln^{n}x-\frac{n}{m+1}\ln^{n-1}x+\frac{n(n-1)}{(m+1)^{2}}\ln^{n-2}x-\cdots+(-1)^{n}\frac{n!}{(m+1)^{n}}\right]+C,$$

即

$$I_{m,n}=\sum_{k=0}^{n}(-1)^{k}\frac{n!}{(n-k)!}\cdot\frac{x^{m+1}}{(m+1)^{k+1}}\ln^{n-k}x+C.$$

#### **习题六-5(2)**

**答案：**

#### **习题六-5(3)**

### **习题六-6**

#### 题目

求下列有理函数的不定积分:

**(1)** $\int \frac{2x}{(x-1)(x+1)^2} \mathrm{d}x$;

**(2)** $\int \frac{2x^2+2x+13}{(x-2)(1+x^2)^2} \mathrm{d}x$;

**(3)** $\int \frac{x^2-x+1}{2x+1} \mathrm{d}x$;

**(4)** $\int \frac{3x+5}{(x^2+2x+2)^2} \mathrm{d}x$;

**(5)** $\int \frac{x+1}{x^3+2x^2-x-2} \mathrm{d}x$;

**(6)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x(x^3+2)}$;

**(8)** $\int \frac{x\mathrm{d}x}{x^3-1}$.

##### **习题六-6(1)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{2x}{(x-1)(x+1)^{2}}\mathrm{d}x,\qquad\frac{2x}{(x-1)(x+1)^{2}}=\frac{a}{x-1}+\frac{b}{x+1}+\frac{c}{(x+1)^{2}}\quad(\text{设})$$

$$2x=a(x+1)^{2}+b(x^{2}-1)+c(x-1)=(a+b)x^{2}+(2a+c)x+(a-b-c)$$

$$a+b=0,\quad 2a+c=2,\quad a-b-c=0\ \Longrightarrow\ a=\frac{1}{2},\ b=-\frac{1}{2},\ c=1$$

$$\text{原式}=\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x-1}-\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x+1}+\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{(x+1)^{2}}=\frac{1}{2}\ln|x-1|-\frac{1}{2}\ln|x+1|-\frac{1}{x+1}+C$$

#### **习题六-6(2)**

**答案：**

$$\int\frac{2x^{2}+2x+13}{(x-2)(x^{2}+1)^{2}}\mathrm{d}x,\qquad\frac{2x^{2}+2x+13}{(x-2)(x^{2}+1)^{2}}=\frac{a}{x-2}+\frac{bx+c}{x^{2}+1}+\frac{dx+e}{(x^{2}+1)^{2}}\quad(\text{设})$$

$$2x^{2}+2x+13=a(x^{2}+1)^{2}+(bx+c)(x-2)(x^{2}+1)+(dx+e)(x-2),\quad\cdots$$

右端展开为

$$(a+b)x^{4}+(c-2b)x^{3}+(2a+b-2c+d)x^{2}+(c-2b-2d+e)x+(a-2c-2e),$$

与左端比较系数得

$$a+b=0,\quad c-2b=0,\quad 2a+b-2c+d=2,\quad c-2b-2d+e=2,\quad a-2c-2e=13,$$

解得 $a=1,\ b=-1,\ c=-2,\ d=-3,\ e=-4$。于是

$$\frac{2x^{2}+2x+13}{(x-2)(x^{2}+1)^{2}}=\frac{1}{x-2}-\frac{x+2}{x^{2}+1}-\frac{3x+4}{(x^{2}+1)^{2}},$$

$$\text{原式}=\int\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x-2}-\int\frac{x+2}{x^{2}+1}\mathrm{d}x-\int\frac{3x+4}{(x^{2}+1)^{2}}\mathrm{d}x$$

$$=\ln|x-2|-\frac{1}{2}\ln(x^{2}+1)-2\arctan x+\frac{3}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{x^{2}+1}-\frac{2x}{x^{2}+1}-2\arctan x+C$$

$$=\ln|x-2|-\frac{1}{2}\ln(x^{2}+1)-4\arctan x+\frac{3-4x}{2(x^{2}+1)}+C.$$

### **习题六-8**

#### 题目

求下列无理函数的不定积分:

**(1)** $\int \frac{\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt[4]{x^3} + 1}\mathrm{d}x$;

**(2)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{1 + \sqrt{x} + \sqrt{x + 1}}$;

**(3)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x^4\sqrt{1 - x^2}}$;

**(4)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x\sqrt{x^2 + x + 1}}$;

**(5)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{(x + 1)\sqrt{x^2 + 4x + 5}}$;

**(6)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x + \sqrt{x^2 + x + 1}}$;

**(7)** $\int \sqrt{\frac{1 - x}{1 + x}}\mathrm{d}x$;

**(8)** $\int \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{x\sqrt{4 - x^2}}$;

**(10)** $\int \sqrt[3]{\frac{2 - x}{2 + x}}\cdot \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{(2 - x)^2}$.

##### **习题六-8(1)**

**答案：**

令 $\sqrt[4]{x}=t$，即 $x=t^{4}$，则 $\sqrt{x}=t^{2}$， $\sqrt[4]{x^{3}}=t^{3}$， $dx=4t^{3}dt$。

$$
\int\frac{\sqrt{x}}{\sqrt[4]{x^{3}}+1}dx=\int\frac{t^{2}}{t^{3}+1}\cdot 4t^{3}dt=4\int\frac{t^{5}}{t^{3}+1}dt
$$

$$
=4\int\left(t^{2}-\frac{t^{2}}{t^{3}+1}\right)dt=\frac{4}{3}t^{3}-\frac{4}{3}\int\frac{d\left(t^{3}\right)}{t^{3}+1}
$$

$$
=\frac{4}{3}t^{3}-\frac{4}{3}\ln\left|t^{3}+1\right|+C=\frac{4}{3}x^{3/4}-\frac{4}{3}\ln\left|1+x^{3/4}\right|+C
$$

#### **习题六-8(2)**

**答案：**

$$
\int\frac{dx}{1+\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{x+1}}=\int\frac{\left(1+\sqrt{x}\right)-\sqrt{x+1}}{\left(1+\sqrt{x}\right)^{2}-\left(\sqrt{x+1}\right)^{2}}dx=\int\frac{1+\sqrt{x}-\sqrt{x+1}}{2\sqrt{x}}dx
$$

$$
=\int\frac{dx}{2\sqrt{x}}+\frac{1}{2}\int dx-\frac{1}{2}\int\sqrt{\frac{x+1}{x}}dx=\sqrt{x}+\frac{x}{2}-\frac{1}{2}\int\sqrt{\frac{x+1}{x}}dx
$$

对最后一个积分，令 $\sqrt{\dfrac{x+1}{x}}=t$，则 $x+1=t^{2}x$， $x=\dfrac{1}{t^{2}-1}$：

$$
\int\sqrt{\frac{x+1}{x}}dx=\int t\cdot\frac{(-1)\cdot 2t}{\left(t^{2}-1\right)^{2}}dt=-2\int\frac{t^{2}}{\left(t^{2}-1\right)^{2}}dt=\cdots\cdots
$$

或令 $x=\tan^{2}\theta$：

$$
\int\sqrt{\frac{x+1}{x}}dx=\int\frac{\sec\theta}{\tan\theta}\cdot 2\tan\theta\sec^{2}\theta\,d\theta=2\int\sec^{3}\theta\,d\theta=\cdots\cdots
$$

$$
\int\sqrt{1+x^{2}}dx=\int\sec^{3}\theta\,d\theta=\frac{1}{2}\left[\sec\theta\tan\theta+\int\sec\theta\,d\theta\right]
$$

$$
=\frac{1}{2}\left[\sec\theta\tan\theta+\frac{1}{2}\ln\left|\frac{1+\sin\theta}{1-\sin\theta}\right|\right]+C=\frac{1}{2}\left[x\sqrt{1+x^{2}}+\ln\left|x+\sqrt{1+x^{2}}\right|\right]+C
$$

两种换元都归结到同一个积分。用 $x=\tan^{2}\theta$ 那条： $\tan\theta=\sqrt{x}$， $\sec\theta=\sqrt{1+x}$，故

$$2\int\sec^{3}\theta\,d\theta=\sec\theta\tan\theta+\ln|\sec\theta+\tan\theta|+C=\sqrt{x^{2}+x}+\ln\left(\sqrt{x+1}+\sqrt{x}\right)+C,$$

即

$$\int\sqrt{\frac{x+1}{x}}dx=\sqrt{x^{2}+x}+\ln\left(\sqrt{x+1}+\sqrt{x}\right)+C.$$

用 $t$ 那条同样得到这个结果： $-2\int\dfrac{t^{2}}{\left(t^{2}-1\right)^{2}}dt=\dfrac{t}{t^{2}-1}+\dfrac{1}{2}\ln\left|\dfrac{t+1}{t-1}\right|+C$ ，而 $t^{2}-1=\dfrac{1}{x}$。

代回第 (2) 题：

$$\int\frac{dx}{1+\sqrt{x}+\sqrt{x+1}}=\sqrt{x}+\frac{x}{2}-\frac{1}{2}\sqrt{x^{2}+x}-\frac{1}{2}\ln\left(\sqrt{x+1}+\sqrt{x}\right)+C.$$

#### **习题六-8(3)**

令 $x=\cos\theta$：

$$
\int\frac{dx}{x^{4}\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}=\int\frac{-\sin\theta\,d\theta}{\cos^{4}\theta\sin\theta}=-\int\frac{d\theta}{\cos^{4}\theta}=-\int\sec^{4}\theta\,d\theta
$$

$$
=-\int\sec^{2}\theta\left(\tan^{2}\theta+1\right)d\theta=-\int\left(\tan^{2}\theta+1\right)d(\tan\theta)=-\frac{1}{3}\tan^{3}\theta-\tan\theta+C
$$

$$
=-\frac{1}{3}\left(\frac{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}{x}\right)^{3}-\frac{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}{x}+C
$$

其中 $\tan\theta=\dfrac{\sqrt{1-x^{2}}}{x}$。

#### **习题六-8(4)**

**答案：**

$$
\int\frac{dx}{x\sqrt{x^{2}+x+1}}=\int\frac{dx}{x\sqrt{\left(x+\frac{1}{2}\right)^{2}+\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^{2}}}
$$

令 $x+\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\tan\theta$，则 $x=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\tan\theta-\dfrac{1}{2}$：

$$
=\int\frac{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sec^{2}\theta\,d\theta}{\left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\tan\theta-\frac{1}{2}\right)\cdot\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\sec\theta}=\int\frac{2\sec\theta}{\sqrt{3}\tan\theta-1}d\theta=2\int\frac{d\theta}{\sqrt{3}\sin\theta-\cos\theta}
$$

$$
=2\int\frac{d\theta}{2\sin(\theta-\alpha)}=\int\csc(\theta-\alpha)d(\theta-\alpha)=\cdots\cdots
$$

由恒等式 $\sqrt{3}\sin\theta-\cos\theta=2\sin(\theta-\alpha)$，其中 $\cos\alpha=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$、 $\sin\alpha=\dfrac{1}{2}$，即 $\alpha=\dfrac{\pi}{6}$。于是

$$\int\csc(\theta-\alpha)\,d(\theta-\alpha)=\ln\left|\tan\frac{\theta-\alpha}{2}\right|+C=\ln\left|\frac{1-\cos(\theta-\alpha)}{\sin(\theta-\alpha)}\right|+C.$$

由 $\tan\theta=\dfrac{2x+1}{\sqrt{3}}$ 得 $\sin\theta=\dfrac{2x+1}{2\sqrt{x^{2}+x+1}}$、 $\cos\theta=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2\sqrt{x^{2}+x+1}}$，于是

$$\cos(\theta-\alpha)=\cos\theta\cos\alpha+\sin\theta\sin\alpha=\frac{x+2}{2\sqrt{x^{2}+x+1}},$$

$$\sin(\theta-\alpha)=\sin\theta\cos\alpha-\cos\theta\sin\alpha=\frac{\sqrt{3}\,x}{2\sqrt{x^{2}+x+1}}.$$

故

$$\text{原式}=\ln\left|\frac{2\sqrt{x^{2}+x+1}-x-2}{\sqrt{3}\,x}\right|+C=\ln\left|\frac{2\sqrt{x^{2}+x+1}-x-2}{x}\right|+C$$

（常数 $\ln\sqrt{3}$ 并入 $C$）。

#### **习题六-8(5)**

**答案：**

令 $x+1=t$：

$$
\int\frac{dx}{(x+1)\sqrt{x^{2}+4x+5}}=\int\frac{dt}{t\sqrt{1+t^{2}}}=\int\frac{t}{\sqrt{1+t^{2}}}\cdot\frac{dt}{t^{2}}=\int\sqrt{1+\frac{1}{t^{2}}}\,d\left(\frac{1}{t}\right)
$$

再令 $\dfrac{1}{t}=\theta$：

$$
=-\int\sqrt{1+\theta^{2}}\,d\theta=-\frac{1}{2}\left[\theta\sqrt{1+\theta^{2}}+\ln\left|\theta+\sqrt{1+\theta^{2}}\right|\right]+C
$$

$$
=\frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{1}{t}\sqrt{1+\frac{1}{t^{2}}}+\ln\left|\frac{1}{t}+\sqrt{1+\frac{1}{t^{2}}}\right|\right]+C=\frac{1}{2}\left[\frac{\sqrt{1+t^{2}}}{t^{2}}+\ln\left|\frac{1+\sqrt{1+t^{2}}}{t}\right|\right]+C=\cdots\cdots
$$

原稿这一条换元链本身不成立，故按题面被积函数重算。令 $u=x+2$，则 $x+1=u-1$、 $x^{2}+4x+5=u^{2}+1$：

$$\int\frac{dx}{(x+1)\sqrt{x^{2}+4x+5}}=\int\frac{du}{(u-1)\sqrt{u^{2}+1}}.$$

再令 $u=\tan\theta$（ $-\dfrac{\pi}{2}<\theta<\dfrac{\pi}{2}$ ），则 $\sqrt{u^{2}+1}=\sec\theta$、 $du=\sec^{2}\theta\,d\theta$：

$$\int\frac{du}{(u-1)\sqrt{u^{2}+1}}=\int\frac{\sec\theta}{\tan\theta-1}d\theta=\int\frac{d\theta}{\sin\theta-\cos\theta}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\int\csc\left(\theta-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)d\theta$$

$$=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ln\left|\frac{1-\cos\left(\theta-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)}{\sin\left(\theta-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)}\right|+C.$$

由 $\tan\theta=u=x+2$ 得 $\sin\theta=\dfrac{x+2}{\sqrt{x^{2}+4x+5}}$、 $\cos\theta=\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{x^{2}+4x+5}}$，于是

$$\sin\left(\theta-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{x+1}{\sqrt{2}\sqrt{x^{2}+4x+5}},\qquad \cos\left(\theta-\frac{\pi}{4}\right)=\frac{x+3}{\sqrt{2}\sqrt{x^{2}+4x+5}},$$

故

$$\text{原式}=\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ln\left|\frac{\sqrt{2}\sqrt{x^{2}+4x+5}-x-3}{x+1}\right|+C.$$

（等价形式：因 $\left(\sqrt{2}\sqrt{x^{2}+4x+5}\right)^{2}-(x+3)^{2}=(x+1)^{2}$，也可写成 $-\dfrac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\ln\left|\dfrac{\sqrt{2}\sqrt{x^{2}+4x+5}+x+3}{x+1}\right|+C$ 。）

#### **习题六-8(6)**

**答案：**

原稿先用 $x+\dfrac{1}{2}=\dfrac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\tan\theta$ 换元，该行已被划去，改用下面的根式代换。

令 $x+\sqrt{x^{2}+x+1}=t$，则 $x^{2}+x+1=t^{2}-2tx$， $x=\dfrac{t^{2}-1}{1+2t}$：

$$
\int\frac{dx}{x+\sqrt{x^{2}+x+1}}=\int\frac{1}{t}d\left(\frac{t^{2}-1}{1+2t}\right)=\int\frac{1}{t}\cdot\frac{2t(1+2t)-2\left(t^{2}-1\right)}{(1+2t)^{2}}dt=\int\frac{1}{t}\cdot\frac{2\left(t^{2}+t+1\right)}{(1+2t)^{2}}dt
$$

$$
\frac{t^{2}+t+1}{t(1+2t)^{2}}=\frac{a}{t}+\frac{b}{1+2t}+\frac{ct+d}{(1+2t)^{2}}\Longrightarrow\cdots\cdots
$$

原稿写的分解式 $\dfrac{a}{t}+\dfrac{b}{1+2t}+\dfrac{ct+d}{(1+2t)^{2}}$ 中， $(1+2t)^{2}$ 是二次因子，其分子只能取常数（否则与前一项重复），故按标准形式设

$$\frac{t^{2}+t+1}{t(1+2t)^{2}}=\frac{a}{t}+\frac{b}{1+2t}+\frac{c}{(1+2t)^{2}},$$

通分比较系数得 $a=1,\ b=-\dfrac{3}{2},\ c=-\dfrac{3}{2}$，即

$$\frac{t^{2}+t+1}{t(1+2t)^{2}}=\frac{1}{t}-\frac{3}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{1+2t}-\frac{3}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{(1+2t)^{2}}.$$

于是

$$\text{原式}=2\int\left[\frac{1}{t}-\frac{3}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{1+2t}-\frac{3}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{(1+2t)^{2}}\right]dt=2\ln|t|-\frac{3}{2}\ln|1+2t|+\frac{3}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{1+2t}+C.$$

回代 $t=x+\sqrt{x^{2}+x+1}$ （取 $x>0$ 时 $t>0$）：

$$\text{原式}=2\ln\left(x+\sqrt{x^{2}+x+1}\right)-\frac{3}{2}\ln\left(2x+2\sqrt{x^{2}+x+1}+1\right)+\frac{3}{2\left(2x+2\sqrt{x^{2}+x+1}+1\right)}+C.$$

#### **习题六-8(7)**

**答案：**

令 $\sqrt{\dfrac{1-x}{1+x}}=t$，则 $1-x=t^{2}+xt^{2}$， $x=\dfrac{1-t^{2}}{1+t^{2}}$，

$$
dx=\frac{-2t\left(1+t^{2}\right)-2t\left(1-t^{2}\right)}{\left(1+t^{2}\right)^{2}}dt=-\frac{4t}{\left(1+t^{2}\right)^{2}}dt
$$

$$
\int\sqrt{\frac{1-x}{1+x}}dx=\int t\cdot\frac{(-4t)}{\left(1+t^{2}\right)^{2}}dt=-4\int\frac{t^{2}}{\left(t^{2}+1\right)^{2}}dt=-4\int\frac{t^{2}+1-1}{\left(t^{2}+1\right)^{2}}dt
$$

$$
=-4\int\frac{dt}{t^{2}+1}+4\int\frac{dt}{\left(t^{2}+1\right)^{2}}=-4\arctan t+4\int\frac{dt}{\left(t^{2}+1\right)^{2}}\quad\left(\text{公式！}\right)=\cdots\cdots
$$

用公式 $\displaystyle\int\frac{dt}{\left(t^{2}+1\right)^{2}}=\frac{t}{2\left(t^{2}+1\right)}+\frac{1}{2}\arctan t+C$：

$$-4\arctan t+4\left[\frac{t}{2\left(t^{2}+1\right)}+\frac{1}{2}\arctan t\right]+C=\frac{2t}{t^{2}+1}-2\arctan t+C.$$

回代 $t=\sqrt{\dfrac{1-x}{1+x}}$：由 $t^{2}+1=\dfrac{2}{1+x}$ 得 $\dfrac{2t}{t^{2}+1}=t(1+x)=\sqrt{1-x^{2}}$；又 $\arctan t=\dfrac{1}{2}\arccos x$。故

$$\text{原式}=\sqrt{1-x^{2}}-\arccos x+C=\sqrt{1-x^{2}}+\arcsin x+C.$$

#### **习题六-8(8)**

**答案：**

$$
\int\frac{dx}{x\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}=\int\frac{dx}{x^{2}\sqrt{\frac{4}{x^{2}}-1}}=\int\frac{-d\left(\frac{1}{x}\right)}{\sqrt{\frac{4}{x^{2}}-1}}
$$

令 $\dfrac{1}{x}=t$：

$$
=-\int\frac{dt}{\sqrt{4t^{2}-1}}=\cdots\cdots
$$

或令 $x=2\sin\theta$：

$$
=\int\frac{2\cos\theta\,d\theta}{2\sin\theta\cdot 2\cos\theta}=\frac{1}{2}\int\csc\theta\,d\theta=\cdots\cdots
$$

第一条路径： $-\displaystyle\int\frac{dt}{\sqrt{4t^{2}-1}}=-\frac{1}{2}\int\frac{dt}{\sqrt{t^{2}-\frac{1}{4}}}=-\frac{1}{2}\ln\left|t+\sqrt{t^{2}-\frac{1}{4}}\right|+C$ ，回代 $t=\dfrac{1}{x}$、 $\sqrt{t^{2}-\dfrac{1}{4}}=\dfrac{\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}{2x}$ 得

$$\text{原式}=-\frac{1}{2}\ln\left|\frac{2+\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}{2x}\right|+C=-\frac{1}{2}\ln\left|\frac{2+\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}{x}\right|+C$$

（常数 $\ln 2$ 并入 $C$）。

第二条路径： $\dfrac{1}{2}\displaystyle\int\csc\theta\,d\theta=\dfrac{1}{2}\ln\left|\tan\dfrac{\theta}{2}\right|+C=\dfrac{1}{2}\ln\left|\dfrac{1-\cos\theta}{\sin\theta}\right|+C$ ，由 $\sin\theta=\dfrac{x}{2}$、 $\cos\theta=\dfrac{\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}{2}$ 得

$$\text{原式}=\frac{1}{2}\ln\left|\frac{2-\sqrt{4-x^{2}}}{x}\right|+C,$$

与上一式相同（因 $\left(2-\sqrt{4-x^{2}}\right)\left(2+\sqrt{4-x^{2}}\right)=x^{2}$）。

#### **习题六-8(9)**

**答案：**

令 $\sqrt{\tan x}=t$，则 $t^{2}=\tan x$， $dx=\dfrac{2t\,dt}{1+t^{4}}$：

$$
\int\sqrt{\tan x}\,dx=\int t\cdot\frac{2t}{1+t^{4}}dt=2\int\frac{t^{2}}{1+t^{4}}dt=2\int\frac{t^{2}\,dt}{t^{4}+2t^{2}+1-2t^{2}}
$$

$$
=2\int\frac{t^{2}\,dt}{\left(t^{2}+1\right)^{2}-\left(\sqrt{2}t\right)^{2}}=2\int\frac{t^{2}\,dt}{\left(t^{2}+\sqrt{2}t+1\right)\left(t^{2}-\sqrt{2}t+1\right)}
$$

$$
=2\int\frac{At+B}{t^{2}+\sqrt{2}t+1}dt+2\int\frac{Ct+D}{t^{2}-\sqrt{2}t+1}dt=\cdots\cdots
$$

比较系数得 $A=-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4},\ B=0,\ C=\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{4},\ D=0$，即

$$\frac{t^{2}}{\left(t^{2}+\sqrt{2}t+1\right)\left(t^{2}-\sqrt{2}t+1\right)}=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\left[-\frac{t}{t^{2}+\sqrt{2}t+1}+\frac{t}{t^{2}-\sqrt{2}t+1}\right].$$

两项都可写成「导数项 + 剩余项」：

$$\frac{t}{t^{2}+\sqrt{2}t+1}=\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{2t+\sqrt{2}}{t^{2}+\sqrt{2}t+1}-\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{t^{2}+\sqrt{2}t+1},$$

$$\frac{t}{t^{2}-\sqrt{2}t+1}=\frac{1}{2}\cdot\frac{2t-\sqrt{2}}{t^{2}-\sqrt{2}t+1}+\frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\cdot\frac{1}{t^{2}-\sqrt{2}t+1},$$

而 $t^{2}\pm\sqrt{2}t+1=\left(t\pm\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{2}\right)^{2}+\dfrac{1}{2}$。积分得

$$\int\sqrt{\tan x}\,dx=\frac{\sqrt{2}}{4}\left[\ln\left(t^{2}-\sqrt{2}t+1\right)-\ln\left(t^{2}+\sqrt{2}t+1\right)+2\arctan\left(\sqrt{2}t-1\right)+2\arctan\left(\sqrt{2}t+1\right)\right]+C,$$

其中 $t=\sqrt{\tan x}$ ，即

$$\int\sqrt{\tan x}\,dx=\frac{1}{2\sqrt{2}}\ln\left|\frac{\tan x-\sqrt{2\tan x}+1}{\tan x+\sqrt{2\tan x}+1}\right|+\frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}\left[\arctan\left(\sqrt{2\tan x}-1\right)+\arctan\left(\sqrt{2\tan x}+1\right)\right]+C.$$

#### **习题六-8(10)**

**答案：**

令 $\dfrac{2-x}{2+x}=t^{3}$，则 $2-x=2t^{3}+xt^{3}$， $x=\dfrac{2\left(1-t^{3}\right)}{1+t^{3}}$。原稿右侧先算出

$$
2-x=2\left[1-\frac{1-t^{3}}{1+t^{3}}\right]=\frac{4t^{3}}{1+t^{3}}
$$

$$
dx=2\cdot\frac{-3t^{2}\left(1+t^{3}\right)-3t^{2}\left(1-t^{3}\right)}{\left(1+t^{3}\right)^{2}}dt=2\cdot\frac{-6t^{2}}{\left(1+t^{3}\right)^{2}}dt
$$

代入：

$$
\int\sqrt[3]{\frac{2-x}{2+x}}\cdot\frac{dx}{(2-x)^{2}}=\int t\cdot\frac{\left(1+t^{3}\right)^{2}}{16t^{6}}\cdot\frac{(-12)t^{2}}{\left(1+t^{3}\right)^{2}}dt=-\frac{3}{4}\int\frac{dt}{t^{3}}
$$

$$
=\frac{3}{8}\cdot\frac{1}{t^{2}}+C=\frac{3}{8}\left(\frac{2+x}{2-x}\right)^{2/3}+C
$$
